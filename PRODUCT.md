# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and technical teams working with terminal coding agents. They keep shuvshow open beside
their terminal to understand visual proposals, inspect generated artifacts, and give feedback without
breaking their working flow.

## Product Purpose

shuvshow gives coding agents a live visual surface. Agents publish diagrams, UI sketches, rendered
prose, diffs, terminal output, images, and code; users watch those posts update and comment back. The
core measure of success is a fast, trustworthy publish-view-comment-revise loop.

## Brand Personality

Playful, precise, alive. shuvshow belongs to the shuv devil family, but remains a serious working tool:
the personality appears in the mark, voice, and moments of live feedback rather than decorative chrome.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboard styling: purple gradients, glass panels, and ornamental glow.
- Theatrical chrome that competes with the work being presented.
- A broad mechanical rename that breaks stable data, configuration, or integration contracts.

## Design Principles

- Show, do not merely describe: the published work is always the visual focus.
- Make the loop feel alive: connection, feedback, and revision state should be immediate and legible.
- Express personality at branded touchpoints, not across every control and container.
- Preserve expert familiarity: standard controls, compact hierarchy, and predictable navigation.
- Keep the fork seam deliberate so upstream improvements remain straightforward to integrate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard and screen-reader operation, use AA text contrast, respect
reduced-motion preferences, and never communicate important state through color alone.
